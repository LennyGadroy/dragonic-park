The Park

Nine wild habitats, one nursery and a research station, all built around the dragons.

How the park works

Dragonic Park is built around the needs of the dragons first. Each zone copies a real wild habitat, and visitors follow safe paths and viewing decks that keep a respectful distance.

Explore the zones

Six zones to discover during your visit.

The Nursery

Where eggs are kept warm and hatchlings take their first flights. Visit the viewing gallery, but please stay quiet.

Fire Cliffs

Volcanic slopes and heated rock pools for dragons that love heat. Watch feeding time from the safe viewing deck.

Frost Bay

A cold coastal zone for ice-loving dragons, with glass tunnels under the water line.

Storm Coast

Open cliffs and strong winds for the fastest flyers. The best place to spot dragons at dusk.

Green Valley

A forest and marsh habitat where two-headed and ground-dwelling dragons live.

Research Station

Our scientists track, treat and study the dragons. Guided visits for schools and researchers.
