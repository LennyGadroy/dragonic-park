About Dragonic Park

Who we are, who we welcome and how we work.

Our mission

Dragonic Park exists to protect endangered dragons, to introduce the public to these animals with clear and reliable information, and to fund that work through an online shop.

Who the park is for

Everyone from 4 to 77 years old is welcome. We design every page and every tour for three main groups.

  • Families and everyone aged 4 to 77
  • Schools and teachers
  • Scientists and researchers

What we do

Conservation

We rescue, treat and release endangered dragons, and protect the habitats they depend on.

Information

We explain dragon species, life cycle, wild habitat and health in simple English.

Services

We offer booking, equipment rental and an online shop that funds our work.

Our commitments

Accessibility policy

  • Large text and high-contrast colours
  • Full keyboard navigation and visible focus
  • Text alternatives for images
  • Simple English, short sentences
  • Works on phones and tablets

AI use policy

  • AI may help translate and suggest tags
  • A human expert checks all scientific content
  • AI-generated images are labelled
  • Visitor data is never used to train AI

Social media strategy

  • TikTok and Instagram: short hatchling videos
  • YouTube: lessons for schools
  • LinkedIn: news for scientists
  • One hashtag: #DragonicPark
