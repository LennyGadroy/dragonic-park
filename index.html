Welcome to Dragonic Park

The first sanctuary where real dragons live, hatch and fly free. Meet them, learn how to care for them, and help us protect the species that are disappearing.

Plan your visitAbout the park
Open every day, from sunrise to dusk. Ages 4 to 77 welcome.

A park built to protect

Dragonic Park is home to more than twenty dragon species, rescued from poachers and from shrinking habitats. Our rangers, vets and scientists look after every dragon, and every ticket and purchase pays for their care.

Meet the dragons

Every dragon belongs to a class. Here is one from each of six classes in our collection.

Night Fury

Strike class

The rarest and fastest dragon in the park. Silent, shy and almost impossible to spot at night.

Deadly Nadder

Sharp class

A colourful bird-like dragon with a spiked tail. Proud and playful once it trusts you.

Gronckle

Boulder class

Small, round and always hungry. Our most popular dragon with young visitors.

Hideous Zippleback

Mystery class

Two heads, one dragon. The heads can disagree, which makes feeding time lively.

Monstrous Nightmare

Stoker class

Bursts into flame when it is excited. Visit it from behind the heat-proof glass.

Terrible Terror

Fear class

A tiny, curious dragon the size of a cat. It loves to hide in visitors’ bags.
